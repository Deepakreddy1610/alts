ABOUT THE EVENT

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at ALITS proudly presents E-UDBHAV 2K26, a two-day ECE Technical Fest aimed at encouraging innovation, enhancing technical competence, and promoting collaborative learning among students. The fest will be conducted on 10th and 11th February 2026 at the ECE Block, ALITS Campus.

EVENT SCHEDULE

On 10th February, a wide range of engaging technical events will be organized, including Knowledge Knockout (technical quiz), Spintron (concept-based ECE event), Power Point Presentation, Electro Talk, Circuit Safari (circuit design and debugging), Technical Treasure Hunt, and Riddles Crypt, which focuses on logical reasoning and technical problem-solving skills.

The schedule for 11th February includes final rounds of selected events, followed by the valedictory function, prize distribution ceremony, and cultural activities, providing a perfect blend of technical excellence and entertainment. Workshops from industry experts will also be conducted throughout the day.
